# {{Product or feature name}}

## Problem
What is broken or missing today, for whom, and what it costs them. Written from the
user's side, not the system's. No solution here.

## Goal
The outcome that means this worked, in one or two sentences.

## What we will build, in plain language
Explain what the feature takes in, what happens to it, and what the user gets back.
Describe the high-level approach without code or unexplained jargon. Distinguish
agreed scope from proposed choices; do not remove an explicitly requested capability
without discussing it and obtaining the user's decision.

## What the user will experience
Walk through one realistic use from the entry point to the finished result. Name the
screens, controls, defaults and optional choices. Explain waiting/progress, success,
empty/unavailable states, errors and retry. Explain how users find previous results
after re-running the feature. Describe how it fits the existing app on narrow screens.

## Users
Who this is for. Where more than one kind of user is affected, say how their needs differ.

## Scope
### In scope
- The things this delivers.

### Out of scope
- The things it deliberately does not, so nobody assumes them.

## User stories
For each: **As a** _who_, **I want** _what_, **so that** _why_.

## Requirements
Numbered, each one testable on its own. Say MUST or SHOULD — a list where everything
is equally important is a list nobody can cut.

## Success measures
How you will know it worked: the number, the event, or the behaviour that changes.

## Open questions
What is still undecided, and who decides it.
